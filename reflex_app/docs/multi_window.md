# Multi-window MapBiomas — territory vs buffer summary

Run: `yvynation_batch_20260525_1655`. Step = 8 years, stages 1985 → 1993 → 2001 → 2009 → 2017 → 2024. Buffer = 10 km external ring. Only the **multi-time-window MapBiomas** analysis was enabled, so the comparison rests entirely on the long-format transitions CSVs.

All deltas are in percentage **points** of each region's own total area (which is held constant by design). "pp" = percentage points.

---

## Bacurizinho (4901) — *Maranhão*

| Region | Total | Forest 1985 | Forest 2024 | Forest Δ | Major 2024 gainers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Territory   | 83,511 ha  | 99.6% | 98.3% | **−1.3 pp** (−1,054 ha) | tiny Forest plantation (0.7%), Mosaic (+0.2 pp) |
| Buffer 10 km | 150,800 ha | 84.4% | **58.9%** | **−25.5 pp** (−38,462 ha) | Pasture +11.8 pp, Mosaic +6.4 pp, Forest plantation +6.8 pp, Soy +3.1 pp |

**Difference:** the protected area is essentially intact (≈99% forest, no agriculture), while its 10-km surroundings lost a quarter of forest cover, replaced mainly by pasture, mosaic-of-uses and new agro-forestry (soy + planted forest).

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Territory   | 135,599 ha | 86.2% | 80.0% | **−6.2 pp** (−8,390 ha) | Pasture +5.8 pp, Mosaic +2.5 pp |

**Difference:** the expansion polygon is itself under noticeable pressure — pasture/mosaic now occupy ~8% of it — but the buffer is converting roughly **3.5× faster** in absolute terms (42 kha vs 8 kha). Savanna also halves outside (−8.5 kha) while it only drops ~3.6 kha inside.

## Betânia — Amazonian floodplain TI

| Region | Total | Forest+Floodable 1985 | Forest+Floodable 2024 | Δ | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Territory   | 123,343 ha | 90.8% | 89.2% | **−1.6 pp** (Floodable −1.4 pp) | River area essentially stable; Wetland +1.8 pp |
| Buffer 10 km | 245,344 ha | 93.1% | 91.7% | **−1.4 pp** (Floodable −1.2 pp) | Tiny pasture (0.3%) and urban (0.1%) appearing |

**Difference:** unique among the set — territory and buffer behave **almost identically**. This is a river-dominated Amazonian landscape where the surroundings are still mostly intact forest, so the buffer is not a frontier here. The +1.8 pp wetland gain inside the TI is the strongest signal, likely flooded forest converting to open wetland.

## Kayapó NE — the hot spot

| Region | Total | Forest 1985 | Forest 2024 | Forest Δ | Major 2024 gainers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Territory   | 1,098,697 ha | 84.9% | 83.9% | **−0.9 pp** (−10,206 ha) | Mining +0.7 pp (8,272 ha) |
| Buffer 10 km | 161,027 ha | 91.0% | **25.8%** | **−65.2 pp** (−105,022 ha) | Pasture +66.1 pp (108,572 ha), Mining +2.1 pp |

**Difference:** by far the most dramatic asymmetry in the whole batch. The TI's NE quadrant lost just 0.9 pp of forest but accumulated 8 kha of internal **mining** (the largest mining footprint of any quadrant or buffer in the run). The buffer collapsed from 91% forest to 26%, with two-thirds now pasture and another 2% mining. The buffer ring around NE is essentially a fully converted agricultural frontier.

## Krenak de Sete Salões — *Atlantic Forest, Minas Gerais*

| Region | Total | Forest 1985 | Forest 2024 | Forest Δ | Major dynamic |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Territory   | 16,607 ha | 43.4% | **49.8%** | **+6.4 pp** (+1,065 ha) | Pasture −4.2 pp, Mosaic −4.9 pp — **forest regrowth** |
| Buffer 10 km | 109,104 ha | 14.4% | 14.6% | **+0.2 pp** | Pasture −1.2 pp, Coffee +1.0 pp (+1,121 ha) |

**Difference:** the only **forest-gaining** territory in the batch — a clear inversion of the Amazonian pattern. The TI is recovering Atlantic Forest at the expense of pasture and mosaic, while its buffer remains heavily pastoral (62% pasture) and is mostly substituting older pasture/mosaic with **coffee plantations**.

## Krenak — *also Atlantic Forest, MG*

| Region | Total | Forest 1985 | Forest 2024 | Forest Δ | Major dynamic |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Territory   | 4,279 ha | 10.3% | **15.5%** | **+5.1 pp** (+220 ha) | Pasture −9.0 pp (−385 ha), Mosaic +3.2 pp |

**Difference:** same direction as Sete Salões but smaller area. The TI is shedding pasture and slowly regaining forest (+5.1 pp); the buffer instead is **intensifying pasture** (+1.4 kha) at the expense of mosaic. Inside vs outside the protected line, the *direction* of change is opposite.

## Cross-cutting takeaways

- **Amazonian TIs (Bacurizinho, Kayapó NW/NE/SE) act as forest enclaves.** Inside they lose <2 pp of forest in 40 years; outside the 10-km ring they lose 20–65 pp, almost entirely to pasture (with soy and planted-forest appearing in Bacurizinho).
- **Kayapó NE/SE buffer rings are completely cleared frontiers** (Forest ≤57% in 2024, pasture ≥30%); NW is closer to a half-cleared frontier; SW is essentially undisturbed.
- **Mining is an internal — not buffer — threat for Kayapó** (NE: +8.3 kha, SE: +10.2 kha inside the TI). The buffers see only modest mining growth.
- **Betânia behaves as an island in still-forested floodplain**: territory and buffer evolve in parallel, both losing ~1.5 pp of Floodable Forest. There is no clear protective effect because there is no pressure yet.
- **Krenak and Krenak de Sete Salões reverse the pattern.** Both Atlantic-Forest TIs are gaining forest (+5.1 to +6.4 pp) while their buffers stagnate or intensify pasture/coffee — consistent with Cerrado/Mata-Atlântica regrowth dynamics where the deforestation front passed decades earlier.
